all around us most of the matter we see exists as mixtures of two or more pure components air sea water soil minerals etc are all examples of such mixtures  mixtures found in our surroundings can be classified as homogeneous that is uniform or non uniform in composition a true solution is homogeneous in composition in it the solute particles can,t be distinguished from solvent even under a microscope . the particle size is usually less than 10 cm  collodial solutions and suspensions are non homogeneous or heterogeneous systems a suspension has solute particles suspended in a solvent the particle size is a larger than 10 cm they are large enough to be seen with the naked eye or under a microscope a collodial solution has dispersed phase consisting of particles which are visible under powerful microscope suspended in dispersing medium like water particle size is usually between 10 cm to 10 cm .
